Who we are

Built around experience in the gas industry.

CRS Gas was founded by Christo Jordaan and has been operating for approximately two months. Although the business is new, Christo brings previous experience from working in gas companies and a strong understanding of the LPG industry.

That experience gives CRS Gas practical knowledge across different parts of the gas sector. The aim is straightforward: make LPG refills convenient for customers while providing a dependable local service.

CRS Gas currently focuses on refills rather than cylinder sales or cylinder exchanges. Customers can order online or contact the team directly to arrange their refill and delivery.
